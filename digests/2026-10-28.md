**Flags d'`UpstreamFixes` nous (8):**
- `trim_boundary_seam_wrap`: `CreateOrientedTrimBoundary` takes a UV gap of whole periods (up to 1% of one) in a closed direction between consecutive curves for a seam crossing and moves the curves after it by the period, instead of filling it with a line across the whole domain that doubles the loop back on itself (WR-491).
- `cone_parallel_line_domains`: `SmCone::IntersectWithCone` clips parallel-cylinder lines to both requested face domains, including overlaps away from the full line's middle (WR-259, #711).
- `shell_empty_cavity_fails`: `SmOffsetExecutive::ShellBrep` of a solid inward fails when the inset leaves no cavity (an empty offset, `SM_ERR_INVALID_INPUT`) or the offset is not a closed solid (`SM_ERR`), instead of handing the input back unhollowed as a success (WR-454, #380; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@fbe43bb).
- `fillet_refuse_ball_not_fitting`: `create_fillet_brep` refuses a blend whose rolling ball does not fit the faces it rolls on (a face curves tighter than the ball at a section of the edge: `BallFitsItsFaces`) before the corners, with `FilletErrorType::NoRoom` (error info code 8, not an upstream code), where the corners fail with the generic status looking for a rail end that is not there (WR-462, #1172; SolidCraft change, diff `2c5baae..e5696ca` in the issue).
- `boolean_lists_virtual_surface_copies`: `SmMerge::BooleanLists` copies its surface operands with the virtual `Copy` (the derived surface, its natural domain and its kind), where upstream's base copy constructor slices every surface to a geometry-less `SmSurface`, so a surface list always fails with `SM_ERR` and the planar 2D path is never taken (WR-196; `plan/upstream-issues/WR-196--booleanlists-slices-derived-surfaces-into-base-smsurface.md`).
- `sphere_small_circle_not_touching`: `SmSphere::IntersectWithPlane` and `IntersectWithSphere` return a single touching point only when the intersection circle's own radius (`sqrt(R^2 - d^2)`, or the two-sphere circle's height, each formed from the gaps to tangency) is within the tolerance, not whenever the gap to tangency is, which collapsed circles of radius up to about `sqrt(2 R tol)` (1e-3 for `R = 1`, `tol = 1e-6`) into one point (WR-79 part 1, #396; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@30d25bf).
- `ssi_tangent_contact_points`: `SmAdvSurfaceIntersector::FindInteriorCurves` seeds a Newton search for isolated tangential contact points (zeros of `S1 - S2` with parallel normals, 4x4 system with the exact Jacobian) from the leaf pairs it cannot trace (normals within 5 or 2 degrees, no local intersection), plus the poles, and adds each contact with a numerically zero gap and normals within 1e-4 rad that is not on a curve already found as a degenerate `TOUCHING` curve (WR-74, #394; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@c26edbc).
- `ssi_trace_tangent_curves_once`: `SmSurfaceIntersector` traces an open tangent curve once: `AddStartPoint` keeps one start point per cluster of tangent boundary start points (`IsSameTangentCluster`), a finished trace removes the clusters at its ends (`DoIntersection`), `TraceCurve` runs its "already traced" test on a first step that snapped onto another start point, and a near-tangent trace stops at the free end of a curve already found instead of running over it (WR-76, #392; the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@0ce09f2).
