- `curve_tight_box_interval_knots`: `SmCurve::CalculateTightBoundingBox` adds only the B-spline knots strictly inside the queried interval (the interval's ends are already in the box).
- `face_tight_box_searches_trim`: `SmFace::CalculateTightBoundingBox` searches a trimmed face's own extreme inside its trim (subdividing the UV domain from the vertex and edge extremes, bounded at depth 16 / 4096 cells), not only the surface's global extreme, so a face whose surface peaks outside the trim is not boxed too small (WR-588, #1799; tangency-labs/smlib@9d44c1b).
- `boolean_skips_faces_apart`: `SmTopologyIntersector::IntersectTopology` skips a face pair whose face boxes lie farther apart than the intersection tolerance (a box that cannot be computed, or holds nothing, rules nothing out), and `SmBrep::MakeEdgeInFaceWithVU`, on a plane, leaves in place without classifying an inner loop whose test point lies outside the box of the new face's outer loop (grown by the edges' tolerances, at the retry tolerance).
- `delete_vertex_joins_seam_iso_curve`: `SmBrep::DeleteTopologicalVertex` joins an edge whose curve is a curve on a B-spline-kind surface along a UV line of constant u or v (the seam an extrusion leaves on its wall) as that surface's iso curve over the edge, the same curve exactly, so the seam of a full-turn face that a union runs on through a covered face is one edge (WR-598; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@829b982).
- `boolean_wire_edge_keeps_faceuse_shell`: `SmBrep::MakeEdgeTopology`, when a new edge joins a wire shell (its start vertex's) to a faceuse shell (its end vertex's), moves the wire shell's wires into the faceuse shell, puts the faceuse shell in the wire shell's place in the region's shell list and deletes the wire shell.
- `boolean_new_face_closure_both_sides`: `SmBrep::TestClosureSplitRegion` asks whether a new face closes a region with `ClosureTraversalFromBothSides`, two breadth-first searches through edge-use radials from the face use and from its mate that stop when they meet or one runs out, instead of `ClosureTraversal`'s depth-first walk from one side that stops only at the mate.
- `stitch_solid_nests_cavities`: `SmStitch::StitchIntoSolid`, when its stitch leaves no lamina edge and more than one region, makes the B-rep a nested solid model (`FindAndSetInfiniteRegion(NULL, SM_VS_NESTEDSOLID)`, with editing disabled): material inside the outer shell, a void inside a shell within it, and so on, each region's outer shell first.
- `ellipse_adjust_step_interval_keeps_turns`: `SmEllipse::AdjustSTEPInterval` converts a new interval end outside the current arc itself: past a full-turn arc it is taken whole turns back into the arc and its NURBS parameter moved by the circle's NURBS length per turn (the other way for an inside-out curve); past a partial arc the arc's NURBS interval continues in proportion to the angle.
