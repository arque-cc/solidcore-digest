- `ellipse_trim_seam_lines_span_cone_height`: `sm_GetEllipseTrimIntervals` evaluates the two seam lines on the cone's whole height (only their sweep from the given domain), as the end circles and the classification are, so a face trimmed along its axis keeps an ellipse whose only seam crossing lies in its trimmed-off part (WR-490, #871; the SolidCraft fork's fix; `plan/upstream-issues/WR-490--ellipse-trim-seam-lines-on-trimmed-domain.md`).
- `fillet_point_step_per_point`: `SmFilletSolver::ComputePointValues` chooses its finite-difference step for each point (1e-3 at a tangent point, 1e-5 otherwise), not once per process as the function-local static `sdStaticStep`, so a blend does not depend on the blends before it ([`Kernel::fillet_point_step`] is then neither read nor set, and an existing latch is kept as it is) (ND-3, #1173; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@0a05860).

- `precise_properties_body_length_scale`: `SmFace::ComputePreciseProperties` scales the volume and moment error bounds by the body size (ten times the root of the estimated area, or the origin's distance when larger), not 1.0.1's tenth of it, so the integration converges at tight accuracies instead of returning `SM_ERR` (WR-472; the SolidCraft fork's tangency-labs/smlib@073d4740).
- `offset_extension_from_base`: An offset surface evaluated beyond its base's domain extends the base itself on every side that can grow at once, each by the domain size times the least power of two that reaches the point and never less than the extension it replaces, so two copies of one offset give the same points whatever was evaluated first (WR-458, #419; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@2d7c7ed).

- `fillet_cleanup_keeps_target_faces`: `DoFilleting`'s `BooleanPostProcess` removes none of the target's own edges, nor an edge between two of its faces (the fork's `m_sPostProcessKeptEdges`/`Faces`), so the patches of a sphere imported in six faces are not merged into a face no patch's parameterization can carry (WR-459, #423; the fork's tangency-labs/smlib@9ddf4a8).
